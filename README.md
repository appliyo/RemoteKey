## ⚡ Highlights

- **Zero-Cloud & 100% Local**: All communication runs strictly over your local Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. No telemetry, no keystroke tracking, no external cloud relays.
- **Ultra-Low Latency (< 1 ms)**: Direct high-speed UDP/TCP socket streaming for instant, lag-free cursor tracking and typing.
- **Universal Smart TV Support**: Control LG webOS, Samsung Tizen, and Android TV alongside your Windows PC.
- **Tablet & S-Pen Drawing**: Turn your Samsung Galaxy Tab or stylus-enabled tablet into a graphics input device with pressure sensitivity.
- **Zero-Install Portable Companion**: `RemoteKey.exe` runs directly without messy installers or background service bloatware.

## 🚀 Quick Start in 2 Steps

1. **Step 1 (Mobile)**: Install RemoteKey from [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.appliyo.remotekey).
2. **Step 2 (Desktop)**: Download the standalone [`RemoteKey.exe`](https://github.com/appliyo/RemoteKey/releases/download/v0.7.0/RemoteKey.exe) on your Windows PC and double-click to run.
